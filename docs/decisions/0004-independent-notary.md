# ADR 0004: Independent notary and witness (blueprint decision D3)

- Status: **Proposed**
- Date: 2026-10-11

## Context
Operator-signed receipts stop below OVERT AAL-4. A witness in a separate account helps, but AAL-4
needs an independent party plus a qualified assessor.

## Options
Glacis-operated notary; another independent attestation provider; internal witness only (pilot).

## Proposal
Internal witness for pilots, clearly labelled. Choose an independent notary before any claim above
operator-controlled assurance. Disclose conflicts of interest for whoever is chosen.
